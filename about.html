About Us

PasaGamit is the student-run marketplace where pre-loved textbooks, uniforms, gadgets, and more find a new home. Bayad na, bago naman buhay — paid for, new life again. Built by students, for students, for a greener campus.

Our mission

We make it easy for students to buy, sell, and trade what they no longer need — within their own school community. No middlemen, no shipping fees, just barangay-style bulletin board energy, digital.

  • Free posting for verified students
  • Campus meet-up points for safer hand-offs
  • Rehoming pre-loved items instead of landfill
  • Student-led, always

Why it matters

Textbooks, uniforms, and gadgets are expensive. PasaGamit lets blockmates help blockmates save money while keeping perfectly good stuff in use longer.
